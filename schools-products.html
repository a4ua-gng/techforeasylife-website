PLAN IT TOGETHER

From first enquiry
to a school proposal.

The school and TEL agree the educational format, delivery responsibilities and commercial scope before a programme is confirmed.

  1. Share the learning context.

    Your school, location, class group, learning goal and preferred period are enough for a first conversation. Do not send pupil lists.

  2. Choose a suitable format.

    Discuss the curriculum, lab, club, project week or educator-led route, and how it fits the existing timetable.

  3. Review the proposal.

    Agree the scope, contents, adult lead, venue requirements, permissions, delivery plan and commercial terms.

  4. Prepare and reflect.

    Use the agreed learning pathway, then review the students’ observations and the learning outcomes with the educator.

BEFORE WE TALK

Useful answers.
A clear next step.

School enquiries are open for 2027. Public individual product availability remains coming soon.

Can TEL fit into a physics lesson?

Yes, the proposal can focus on topics such as forces, motion, energy or measurement. The educator selects the appropriate level and learning objective; this is not an NCERT endorsement or a new qualification.

Do we need to create a new subject?

No. The five formats are designed around existing lessons, labs, clubs and school occasions. The final format is agreed with your institution.

Does SafeFlight approve a launch?

No. The public version is a learning demonstration. A school-appointed adult retains practical control, and real activity needs the appropriate permissions, environment and agreed responsibilities.

Can individuals order the kit now?

Individual public availability is coming soon. The current route is a school or institutional enquiry, not an online checkout or a paid reservation.

What should we include in a first enquiry?

A school name, city, adult contact, broad class group, preferred period and learning goal. Do not send students’ personal information through the general enquiry form.
