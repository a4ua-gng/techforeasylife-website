02 / CONTINUITY & PEOPLE

When things change.

Keep the decision owner, handover and review date visible to the people doing the work.

Founder coverage and temporary delegation +

When one founder is away, the available founder takes over operating decisions for the agreed period. HR records the acting scope and end date. When both are away, Tanishka covers TeMark, Maverick covers Finance and Humaira covers HR. The founders name an acting owner for TEL Education before planned overlap. If unexpected overlap leaves Education uncovered, HR coordinates urgent handoffs and defers decisions that need a portfolio owner.

Acting leads keep approved work moving. Ownership changes, permanent appointments, pay decisions and commitments outside approved limits go to the designated approver. When leadership returns, HR closes the handover and records decisions made during cover.

Pausing or replacing a team lead +

The Founder can take over TeMark’s lead duties and pause or withdraw delegated lead authority when delivery needs it. Apply the same continuity process in Finance through its portfolio sponsor, and in HR through the Co-founder. When a decision affects the Co-founder’s portfolio, get their consent or use a documented acting handover.

Record the reason, scope, acting lead and review date privately with HR. Give the affected person a chance to respond. During a pause they may carry agreed member-level duties. Restore lead authority or communicate a reviewed change in writing.

HR communications and escalation +

HR issues company announcements, runs HR discussions, maintains the leave and decision records, and follows up on handovers. Leads pass on team updates and flag risks. Members can contact HR directly about a sensitive issue. If the concern involves HR, route it privately to a founder without a conflict.

December 2026 compensation review +

From December 2026, responsibilities and acting duties can inform prospective compensation reviews. Finance verifies the calculation, HR documents the review and shares any proposed change privately, and the authorised approver signs off in writing under the engagement terms. The chart and leave log record roles and availability. They do not trigger automatic deductions or changes to earned pay.
