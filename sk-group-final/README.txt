SK GROUP X — PHOTOGRAPHY EDITION

Open index.html, or publish this folder on a static host. Navigation,
photo gallery, the business directory and the enquiry composer use
local files. The enquiry form opens a draft to skgroupx@gmail.com in
the visitor's email app. The visitor reviews and sends it there.

The site uses supplied photographs and reusable photography sourced
from Wikimedia Commons. Attribution and licence links are listed in
photo-credits.html. The licensed derivatives in assets/ retain their
source photographs' CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. Sector photos are labelled
where they do not show an SK Group X property or product.

Business entries and public links are in data.js. The supplied
9 AM–8 PM is shown as group enquiry hours. No unconfirmed branch
addresses or social accounts were added.
