A NOTE FROM THE FOUNDER   ↘

I began with the kind of idea that fits on a notebook page: make technology more useful in everyday life. The first smart-home concept eventually became Tech for Easy Life. Along the way I learned that building a company isn't just about having an idea. It's about testing it, listening to people, finding the right team and doing the work when nobody is watching.

FLAGSHIP VENTURE
01 / FOUNDER & CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Tech for
Easy Life.

What began with a student technology project grew into TEL, an initiative developing hands-on, curriculum-connected STEM learning experiences. Our model rocketry education work combines physical kits, guided learning and the SafeFlight safety-learning concept. The goal is to move learning beyond the textbook while keeping institutions and educators at the centre.
