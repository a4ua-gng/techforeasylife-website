Refund & Cancellation Policy

Draft for review · 30 October 2026

Policy draft for the proposed website. Confirm the legal operator, business address, grievance contact and operational commitments before publication.

No payment for making an enquiry

This website does not take payment, sell products through a checkout or charge for sending an enquiry. TEL Education’s individual product launch is coming soon. School enquiries for 2027 do not create a paid booking.

TeMark projects and retainers

For a paid project agreed separately, the written proposal or service agreement must identify the contracting operator, scope, start date, payment schedule and cancellation or refund terms before a payment is accepted. Existing signed agreements are not replaced by this draft website policy, subject to mandatory law.

Proposed approach for new agreements: a cancellation request is reviewed against work already performed, approved non-recoverable third-party costs and any unearned prepaid amount. Do not assume every advance is non-refundable. Any agreed deductions and refund amount should be explained in writing. Statutory remedies for deficient, misdescribed or undelivered services remain available where applicable.

School programmes and future product sales

Institution-specific proposals must set out postponement, cancellation, delivery and any refund arrangements before acceptance. Product returns, faulty-item remedies, warranty, shipping, return costs and refund timing must be published and agreed before online product orders are opened.

A blanket “no refunds” statement does not override rights under applicable consumer law. TEL should not collect an online order or payment until the relevant terms and business details are complete.

How to raise a request

Email techforeasylife.operations@gmail.com with the project or invoice reference and a short description. Do not send full bank details or identity documents in an initial request. TEL should confirm receipt, explain the applicable agreement and provide a written decision and, where a refund is accepted, the method and expected processing date.

The grievance officer and contact route must be confirmed on the Business details page before publication. This proposed policy needs approval by TEL’s authorised contracting operator.
