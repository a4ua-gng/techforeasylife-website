Privacy Policy

Draft for review · 30 October 2026

Policy draft for the proposed website. Confirm the legal operator, business address, grievance contact and operational commitments before publication.

Who this covers

This policy describes the proposed TEL website for TEL Education and TeMark by TEL in Guwahati, Assam, India. Our business and privacy contacts are listed on the Business details page.

The information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, enquiry topic and a short message. Your organisation is optional. These fields stay in the page while you compose your message; they are not uploaded to an enquiry database or stored in browser storage by this website.

Prepare email enquiry opens a draft in your own email app. TEL receives the contents only if you send the email. Copy enquiry copies the draft only after you select that button. Your email provider handles the draft under its own privacy terms.

If you send an enquiry, TEL uses the details to reply, discuss the requested service and manage any resulting project. Enquiry consent is separate from marketing, and does not sign you up to promotional messages.

Careers and children’s information

For a careers enquiry, share relevant experience, availability and work links. Do not send identity documents, date of birth, bank details, health information or other sensitive records at this stage. Application information is used to review the enquiry and contact you about it.

School enquiries should come from an authorised adult. Do not upload or email pupil lists or children’s personal details through the general enquiry route. Separate arrangements and any legally required guardian consent must be established before a programme processes children’s information.

Optional weather lookup

SafeFlight’s city weather lookup is optional and makes no request until you enter a city, select the weather consent checkbox and submit. It shares the city query, the selected city’s approximate coordinates and the normal network information, including IP address, with Open-Meteo. It does not request GPS access.

Open-Meteo processes its service requests under its own terms and privacy information. You can use an approved weather source instead. A weather result does not authorise a launch.

Service providers and external links

Site delivery and security services may process IP addresses, browser information and request logs needed to operate the website. TEL email is handled through Google’s email services. Necessary processing can take place in countries where those providers operate.

YouTube and Instagram are ordinary links, not automatically loaded players, feeds or advertising pixels. Those services receive your visit only when you follow their links. Fonts and portfolio images are served with the site. This build includes no advertising pixel, session replay or visitor analytics script.

Retention and access

Proposed retention for approval: delete or anonymise unconverted routine enquiries within 180 days of the last response, and unsuccessful recruitment enquiries within 90 days after the recruitment decision. Active project correspondence, invoices and records needed for a complaint or legal obligation may be retained longer for that purpose.

Limit access to people who need the information to handle the enquiry or project. Do not use enquiry details to build advertising audiences, sell contact lists or train a public model. These operational commitments must be confirmed by TEL before launch.

Your choices and contact

You may choose not to send an enquiry. To withdraw enquiry consent, correct information, request deletion or raise a privacy concern, email techforeasylife.operations@gmail.com. Include enough context to identify the message without sending an identity document. Requests may be subject to records TEL must retain by law.

The Cookie Policy explains browser storage. Material changes should be shown here with a revised date. This draft does not claim privacy certification or complete legal compliance.
