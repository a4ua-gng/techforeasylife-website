# Scheduled clock-ins

The company default is **17:00 IST, Monday–Friday**, effective **16 November 2026**. Every active role inherits it, including founders and new hires. HR can set individual exceptions. Late clock-in remains available; notifications never suspend an account.

`GET /api/schedules` returns `{policy,pending_policy,schedules,today,next_shift,holidays}`. Policy is `{timezone,start_time,weekdays:[1..7],enabled,effective_date,reminder_minutes:15,grace_minutes:10,escalation_minutes:30}`. Each schedule is `{user_id,name,department,manager_id,inherited,start_time,weekdays,enabled,effective_date,note,today,next_shift,pending_change}`. Weekday 1 is Monday, 7 Sunday. HR/founders see everyone, departmental heads their team and themselves, employees themselves. `today` and `next_shift` at the top level are the signed-in member's values. Pending policy/change fields show the earliest future version, or null.

Today is `{date,status,reason,scheduled_at,first_clock_in,minutes_late}`. Status is `upcoming|due|missing|on_time|late|exempt|not_scheduled`. Grace ends ten minutes after the scheduled start; actual `minutes_late` measures from the scheduled start. A member clocking within grace is marked on_time. `next_shift` is `{date,scheduled_at,start_time}` or null and excludes known leave, exam/unavailable periods and holidays.

- `PUT /api/schedules/:user_id` body `{start_time:'HH:mm',weekdays:[1,2,3,4,5],enabled:true,effective_date:'YYYY-MM-DD',note:''}` sets an individual override. HR/founders only; response `{schedule}`.
- `DELETE /api/schedules/:user_id` restores company inheritance. HR/founders only. If the inherited start has already passed today and there is no real clock-in or exemption, this returns409 and preserves the override. Set a future personal schedule or reset before the next company start.
- `PATCH /api/schedules/policy` updates supplied global policy fields, including reminder/grace/escalation minute values. Founders/HR lead only; response `{policy}`. Timezone stays Asia/Kolkata.
- `POST /api/holidays` body `{date:'YYYY-MM-DD',title:'Company holiday'}` creates an organisation holiday; HR/founders only. Response `{holiday}`.
- `DELETE /api/holidays/:id` removes a holiday; HR/founders only. Removing today's holiday returns409 if it would turn an exempt team member into a due/missing clock-in after their working start. Future and non-working-day holidays can still be removed.

Individual changes need a valid future date or a start still in the future today. For policy changes, choose today before its start, or a future date. Changes are audited and the affected member receives an in-app notification. Policy and personal schedule versions preserve today's applicable configuration until the replacement effective date. Schedule notes are visible to authorised departmental heads and HR; keep medical/personal details out of these notes.

Cron should call `runAttendanceReminders(env, atISO?)` every five minutes. It sends a personal reminder within 15 minutes before start, a due notice from start until grace ends, and one missed-clock-in notice after grace. After 30 minutes without a real clock-in it also notifies the reporting manager and active HR, excluding the member themself. Unique dedupe keys prevent repeats. Approved leave, manual On Leave status, exam/unavailable calendar entries, company holidays, disabled schedules, suspended/exited accounts and approved attendance corrections are exempt. No alerts are backfilled for dates before activation or a new account created after that day's start. Notification delivery is in-app; no external Slack/email/push connection is implied.

Actual start detection uses `clock_in_events`, recorded only by an explicit clock-in action. Automatic attendance rollover at local midnight cannot satisfy the next working day's clock-in. Members should close an overnight session and explicitly clock in for their next working day. Approved corrections exempt the date from missed-clock-in alerts.
