About this concept

A presentation website for SK GROUP (OPC), with the seven showroom photographs supplied for the brief. Business names and relationships follow the client brief and should be confirmed before official use.

Additional product and lifestyle images illustrate the brands in the showcase. They are not photographs of local SK Group events, staff or stock. The care photograph is an illustrative stock image.

Image collections: Royal Enfield · TVS Motor · Yamaha Motor India · Montra Electric · Ashok Leyland · Kaboompics / Pexels.

The two GS Auto addresses and phone numbers are from Royal Enfield’s public dealer directory. No photograph is assigned to a specific GS Auto branch. This demo does not take bookings or enquiries. Brand names and imagery belong to their respective owners.
