HR & OUTREACH / LEAVE PROCEDURE

Leave & working hours.

Submit planned leave in writing at least 48 hours before it starts. Choose your role below to see who reviews it, who approves it and who needs an update.

✳Leave approval routes

01 / TEAM MEMBER

Inform HR first. Then notify your lead.

Use the private HR leave channel or ticket. Your lead records the absence and confirms how your work will be covered.

  1. 01

    Write to HR

    Include dates and times, a brief reason category, affected work and your coverage plan. Send at least 48 hours ahead.

  2. 02

    Notify your lead

    Share the dates, affected tasks and proposed handover. Your lead confirms coverage and updates HR.

  3. 03

    Get written confirmation

    HR confirms the decision. Get the applicable founder’s written approval for an assigned offline shift, a swap or leave longer than one month.

  4. 04

    Hand over & log

    Finish your handover before leaving. HR records the decision and includes it in the founders’ leave report.

02 / TEAM LEAD

Discuss your leave with the founders.

Discuss your proposed dates and acting lead with both founders. Copy HR on the written request.

  1. 01

    Discuss & submit

    Give at least 48 hours’ notice. Include deliverables, risks, client or school commitments and an acting lead who accepts the handover.

  2. 02

    Consult HR & Co-founder

    The Founder checks staffing and workload with HR and the Co-founder. When the HR lead is applying, another authorised reviewer handles their HR intake.

  3. 03

    Founder approves

    The Founder or acting founder issues the written decision after consultation. HR records the dates, acting scope and any conditions.

  4. 04

    Notify the team

    Share approved dates, acting lead and escalation contact. Give the acting lead a written handover before leave starts.

03 / FOUNDER OR CO-FOUNDER

Arrange cover. Get HR approval. Notify TEL.

Both founders follow this route for their own leave. For planned overlap, agree who covers each department before either absence starts.

  1. 01

    Notify the other founder

    Share the proposed dates at least 48 hours ahead. Agree acting ownership, limits, handover and any affected commitments.

  2. 02

    Submit to HR

    Send the leave request and coverage plan. HR checks that urgent work and escalations have a named point of contact.

  3. 03

    HR approves & records

    HR gives written approval and logs the cover arrangement. If both founders are away, use the team lead coverage in the hierarchy.

  4. 04

    Notify everyone

    After approval, share the dates, acting contacts and escalation route in the company announcements channel.

01 / AVAILABILITY

Working hours & calls.

All times are in Indian Standard Time (IST).

ONLINE

Flexible hours

Online work has no fixed working hours. Agree deadlines and any live meeting in advance. Flag delays and availability changes to your lead.

OFFLINE

Follow the posted roster

Use the official shift schedule already shared with the team. Notify the shift lead about delays or early departure. Ask HR to record approved changes.

12:00 A.M. IST · MIDNIGHT

Emergency calls only

After midnight, call only for an emergency that needs immediate action. Send routine matters as a message for the next day.

02 / LEAVE SOP

From request to return.

Apply these rules to members, team leads and founders. Use the written route above for the final decision.

What to include in a planned request +

Send your name, role and team, start and end dates and times in IST, return time, a brief reason category, affected shifts and deadlines, the handover links, and who has accepted cover. Mention whether you can be reached for urgent issues. Raise known school or exam conflicts early.

Share personal details privately with HR only when needed. Team notices should contain availability, coverage and the escalation contact.

When leave becomes official +

HR records the request as pending, then updates it after the required written decision. For planned leave, keep the current assignment until written approval arrives. Approved roster absences are excused. HR and the lead update the roster after approval. Changes, extensions, cancellations and swaps go through the same channel. Swaps and replacements need written founder approval.

For leave longer than one month, HR escalates to the relevant founder before issuing a decision. If the usual reviewer has a conflict, HR routes it to the alternate authorised reviewer.

Illness, emergencies and late notice +

For illness or an emergency, contact HR and the lead as soon as you safely can. Give your estimated availability and flag urgent work. HR logs the exception and the lead arranges immediate coverage. The usual written review follows when practical.

For an unexpected no-show, HR checks welfare privately, sends a written follow-up and gives 24 hours to reply when reasonable. Try one direct call within normal contact hours, or after midnight only for an emergency. Reassign urgent work, log an unresolved case as unnotified leave, and give the person a chance to respond before any further review.

Handover, coverage and return +

Hand over open work, due dates, approved file links, blockers, client or school commitments, and the escalation contact. The acting owner confirms receipt. Keep credentials in the approved access system. When returning, confirm availability, take work back with the acting owner and ask HR to close the record.

The posted roster still applies to everyone outside the approved leave or travel group. When both founders are away, consult the coverage rules.

HR’s report to both founders +

HR sends both founders a weekly leave summary with request IDs, teams, dates, decisions, affected shifts, coverage and items awaiting action. Flag coverage gaps, overlapping leadership leave or urgent issues as soon as they arise. Keep personal reasons in the restricted HR record.

04 / RELATED TEL PROCEDURES

Where the SOPs connect.

Use these routes when a leave case touches another team process.

Attendance, conduct and handovers +

Follow the posted shift roster and dress code, arrive on time and tell the shift lead promptly about an unavoidable delay. Follow the assigned lead, protect client information and equipment, and hand over unfinished work. Use the dated roster for approved exceptions.

Warnings, strikes and inactivity +

HR keeps the record and contacts the person privately. Use the emergency or unnotified-leave steps above first. Refer repeated issues to the relevant founder for a documented review and give the person a chance to respond.

Recruitment, promotion and exit +

Ask HR to coordinate the applicable SOP and confirm who can approve the change. Use an acting handover when someone is temporarily away. Use the formal appointment, promotion or exit process for lasting changes.

Conflicts and complaints +

Raise concerns privately with HR or a founder without a conflict. Keep leave records separate from a complaint and confirm any temporary reporting or cover arrangement in writing.

Finance, contribution incentives and pay +

Finance reviews documented payments and withdrawals with the authorised approver. Contribution incentives use the communicated criteria. Record approved extra work under the applicable rules. Route changes to compensation through Finance, HR and the authorised approver. See the December compensation review.
